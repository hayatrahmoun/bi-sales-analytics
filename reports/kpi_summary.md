| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total sales | $5.5M |
| Total profit | $812K |

| Average order value | $1,839 |

## Key findings
1. **Discounts above 20% destroy profit**: those lines earn a -15.4% margin
   and lose $144K in total.
2. **Technology is the largest category but has the lowest margin** (6.6%),
   while Furniture earns 23.6%.
3. **November and December are the peak**: they generate 28%
   of sales, so stock and staffing should be planned around them.
4. Sales grew 11.6% year over year, but the margin slipped from
   14.9% to 14.6%.

## Recommendations
- Cap discounts at 20% (or require approval above it), especially on Technology products.
- Review Technology pricing and supplier costs to lift the margin.
- Prepare inventory and marketing for the Nov-Dec peak.
